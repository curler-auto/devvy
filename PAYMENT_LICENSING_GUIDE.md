# Payment Aggregator License - Cost, Process & Alternatives

## 1. Understanding Payment Aggregator (PA) License

### What is a Payment Aggregator?
A Payment Aggregator is an entity that facilitates payment transactions between merchants and customers by aggregating multiple merchants under one umbrella.

**RBI Definition (2020):**
> "Payment Aggregator means a person who facilitates payment transactions between the customer and the merchant by providing the technology infrastructure."

### Do You Need a PA License?

**You NEED a PA License if:**
- ❌ You collect payments from users in YOUR name
- ❌ You hold customer funds (even temporarily)
- ❌ You settle funds to merchants/vendors
- ❌ You process >50 lakhs/month in payment volume

**You DON'T NEED a PA License if:**
- ✅ You use an existing PA (Razorpay, Stripe, PayU)
- ✅ You use sub-merchant model (Razorpay Route, Stripe Connect)
- ✅ Vendors receive payments directly (you just facilitate)

## 2. PA License Requirements (RBI Guidelines)

### Eligibility Criteria

**For Companies:**
- ✅ Incorporated in India (Private Ltd or Public Ltd)
- ✅ Minimum Net Worth: **₹15 Crores** (at the time of application)
- ✅ Minimum Net Worth: **₹25 Crores** by March 31 of 3rd financial year
- ✅ No adverse history with RBI/SEBI/IRDA
- ✅ Fit and proper criteria for directors/promoters

**For Individuals:**
- ❌ Not allowed - Only companies can apply

### Capital Requirements

| Stage | Net Worth Required |
|-------|-------------------|
| **At Application** | ₹15 Crores |
| **Year 1** | ₹15 Crores |
| **Year 2** | ₹20 Crores |
| **Year 3 onwards** | ₹25 Crores |

**Source of Capital:**
- Equity from promoters/investors
- Cannot use debt/loans for net worth
- Must be verified by auditors

## 3. PA License Application Process

### Step-by-Step Process

#### Step 1: Pre-Application (1-2 months)
- [ ] Incorporate company (if not already)
- [ ] Raise capital to meet ₹15 Cr net worth
- [ ] Appoint qualified directors
- [ ] Setup IT infrastructure
- [ ] Develop payment platform
- [ ] Hire compliance team
- [ ] Engage legal/CA firms

**Cost:** ₹50 lakhs - ₹1 crore

#### Step 2: Application Submission (1 month)
- [ ] Prepare detailed application
- [ ] Business plan and projections
- [ ] IT security audit report
- [ ] Background verification of directors
- [ ] Financial statements (audited)
- [ ] Board resolutions
- [ ] Pay application fee

**Application Fee:** ₹1 lakh (non-refundable)

**Documents Required:**
1. Certificate of Incorporation
2. Memorandum & Articles of Association
3. Board Resolution
4. Business Plan (5 years)
5. IT Security Audit Report (CERT-In empaneled auditor)
6. Financial Statements (last 3 years)
7. Net Worth Certificate
8. KYC of Directors/Promoters
9. Background verification reports
10. Details of technology infrastructure

#### Step 3: RBI Review (6-12 months)
- RBI reviews application
- May ask for clarifications
- Site inspection possible
- Interview with management
- Technical evaluation

**No additional cost** (unless you hire consultants)

#### Step 4: In-Principle Approval (if granted)
- Valid for 18 months
- Must comply with all conditions
- Setup operations
- Implement systems
- Hire team

**Cost:** ₹1-2 crores (operations setup)

#### Step 5: Final License (after 18 months)
- Submit compliance report
- RBI inspection
- Final approval
- License granted

**Annual License Fee:** ₹5 lakhs/year

## 6. Alternative: Sub-Merchant Model (RECOMMENDED)

### What is Sub-Merchant Model?

Instead of becoming a PA yourself, you partner with an existing licensed PA (Razorpay, Stripe) and onboard vendors as "sub-merchants" under your account.

### How It Works

```
User Payment (₹1,000)
         ↓
Razorpay/Stripe (Licensed PA)
         ↓
Automatic Split:
├─> Vendor (Sub-merchant): ₹850
└─> Your Platform: ₹150 (commission)
```

### Available Solutions

#### Option 1: Razorpay Route (India)
**What it is:** Razorpay's marketplace solution

**Features:**
- Onboard unlimited vendors as sub-merchants
- Automatic payment splitting
- Instant settlements to vendors
- KYC verification handled by Razorpay
- Compliance managed by Razorpay

**Cost:**
- Setup fee: ₹0 (free)
- Transaction fee: 2% + ₹3 (standard Razorpay)
- Route fee: +0.5% (additional for split payment)
- **Total: 2.5% + ₹3 per transaction**

**Settlement:**
- Instant to vendors (T+0)
- Your commission auto-collected
- No working capital needed

**Requirements:**
- Your company must be registered
- GST registration required
- Basic KYC documents
- No minimum net worth

**Onboarding Time:** 2-3 days

## 9. Regulatory Compliance (Sub-Merchant Model)

### What You Still Need to Do

Even with sub-merchant model, you need:

1. **Company Registration**
   - Private Limited Company
   - Cost: ₹10,000 - ₹50,000
   - Time: 7-15 days

2. **GST Registration**
   - Mandatory for marketplace
   - Cost: Free (DIY) or ₹5,000 (CA)
   - Time: 3-7 days

3. **PAN & TAN**
   - For company
   - Cost: Free
   - Time: 1-2 days

4. **Bank Account**
   - Current account
   - Cost: ₹0 (most banks)
   - Time: 3-5 days

5. **Terms & Conditions**
   - User terms
   - Vendor terms
   - Privacy policy
   - Cost: ₹25,000 - ₹1,00,000 (lawyer)

6. **Data Protection**
   - SSL certificate
   - Data encryption
   - Privacy compliance
   - Cost: ₹10,000 - ₹50,000

**Total Compliance Cost (Sub-Merchant): ₹50,000 - ₹2,50,000**

## 12. Risk Analysis

### PA License Risks
- ❌ Huge capital requirement (₹15-25 Cr)
- ❌ 2-3 year approval process
- ❌ May get rejected (no refund)
- ❌ Heavy compliance burden
- ❌ RBI audits and inspections
- ❌ Penalties for non-compliance
- ❌ Liability for fraud/chargebacks
- ❌ Need to maintain net worth

### Sub-Merchant Model Risks
- ⚠️ Dependent on PA (Razorpay/Stripe)
- ⚠️ PA can change terms/fees
- ⚠️ PA can suspend your account
- ⚠️ Limited customization
- ⚠️ Higher transaction fees
- ✅ But: Minimal financial risk
- ✅ But: No compliance burden
- ✅ But: Quick to launch
